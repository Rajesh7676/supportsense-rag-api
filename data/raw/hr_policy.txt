Title: HR Leave Policy 2024

Section 1: Casual Leave
- Every full-time employee gets 12 days of casual leave per year.
- Casual leave cannot be carried forward to the next year.

Section 2: Sick Leave
- Employees get 10 days of sick leave per year.
- A medical certificate is required for leaves more than 2 days.

Section 3: Work From Home
- Work from home is allowed for a maximum of 2 days per week with manager approval.
