Title: IT Helpdesk FAQ

VPN Issues:
- If VPN is not connecting, restart your laptop and try again.
- If the issue persists, raise a ticket on the IT portal.

Laptop Replacement:
- Laptops older than 3 years are eligible for replacement.
- Raise a hardware request and your manager must approve it.

Password Reset:
- Use the self-service password reset portal.
- If locked out, call the IT helpdesk.
